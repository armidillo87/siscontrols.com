## 1. Wholesale Sourcing Strategy & Special Pricing Agreements (SPA)

### Multiplier Mechanics
Wholesale electrical equipment pricing is quoted as a fractional decimal multiplier against the manufacturer's published list price:
* **Standard Retail / Walk-in:** 0.85 to 1.00 of List (0 to 15% discount).
* **Electrical Contractor Tier:** 0.55 to 0.70 of List (30 to 45% discount).
* **OEM / Panel Builder (Column 4):** 0.40 to 0.52 of List (48 to 60% discount).
* **Project-Specific Special Pricing Agreement (SPA):** 0.28 to 0.38 of List (62 to 72% discount).

*Rule of Thumb:* By registering client projects (e.g., Micron Megafab, Meta Kuna, or regional food processors) with distributor factory reps, Brandon secures project-specific SPAs that lower BOM acquisition costs by 15% to 25% below standard contractor pricing, creating an automatic 35% to 50% gross margin spread on equipment billing.

## 3. Supplement SA Substitution Decision Protocol

When lead times on primary Allen-Bradley components exceed client deadlines, use this 4-step compliance protocol to execute drop-in substitutions without invalidating UL 508A listing:

1. **Verify NRTL Listing Category:** Confirm the proposed replacement carries an identical UL Category Code (e.g., DIVQ for Molded Case Circuit Breakers, NKCR for Auxiliary Devices, NMTR for Power Supplies).
2. **Interrupting Rating & Let-Through Check:** Ensure the replacement component possesses an interrupting rating equal to or higher than the target SCCR and that peak let-through current ($I_{peak}$) does not exceed the downstream device withstand rating.
3. **Manufacturer Substitution Table Cross-Check:** Confirm the replacement component is listed in UL 508A Table SA1.1 or the specific drive/starter manufacturer coordinated high-fault combination listing.
4. **Submittal Engineering Notification:** Issue an official submittal package noting: *"Substitution executed pursuant to UL 508A Supplement SA. Functional, physical, and SCCR ratings equal or exceed base specification."*
