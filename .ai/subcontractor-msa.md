### Recitals

WHEREAS, Contractor provides industrial automation engineering, component sourcing, project management, and control systems integration to industrial, commercial, and mission-critical clients; and

WHEREAS, Subcontractor owns and operates a certified panel assembly facility listed under UL 508A (Standard for Industrial Control Panels) by Underwriters Laboratories or equivalent Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory (NRTL); and

WHEREAS, Contractor desires from time to time to retain Subcontractor to perform build-to-print assembly, wiring, and serialized UL 508A listing of industrial control panels and equipment skids pursuant to individual Statements of Work (SOW);

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### Section 1: Scope of Work and Statement of Work (SOW)

1.1 **Issuance of SOW:** For each project, Contractor will issue a written Statement of Work ("SOW") detailing the panel specifications, mechanical drawings, electrical schematics, Bill of Materials ("BOM"), customer quality standards, required delivery dates, and agreed fixed pricing or shop labor hours.

1.2 **Precedence:** In the event of a conflict between the terms of this Agreement and any SOW, the specific terms of the SOW shall govern for that project only, provided that Sections 4 (Anti-Indemnity), 5 (Warranty), and 8 (Insurance) of this Agreement may not be modified without express written reference to this Master Agreement.

1.3 **Build-to-Print Requirement:** Subcontractor agrees to fabricate, wire, and label all panels strictly in accordance with Contractor-approved schematics and BOM. Subcontractor shall make no engineering modifications, component substitutions, wire gauge adjustments, or terminal reassignments without prior written Engineering Change Order ("ECO") authorized by Contractor.

---

### Section 2: Quality Assurance, Testing, and UL 508A Certification

2.1 **UL 508A Listing Mark:** Subcontractor certifies that its fabrication facility maintains an active, unrevoked UL 508A Manufacturer Technical Representative ("MTR") certification and listing agreement. Subcontractor shall affix its authorized, serialized UL 508A listing label to every panel completed under this Agreement, confirming compliance with NFPA 70 Article 409 and UL 508A standards.

2.2 **Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT):** Prior to release or shipment, Subcontractor must perform and pass a comprehensive Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) on 100% of panels produced. The FAT shall include:
(a) Point-to-point electrical continuity verification against schematics.  
(b) Megohmmeter insulation resistance test.  
(c) Dielectric withstand (hi-pot) test in strict compliance with UL 508A Section 65.  
(d) Live 480V/120V/24VDC functional power and I/O loop simulation.  
(e) Short-Circuit Current Rating (SCCR) verification confirming all components match approved submittal values.  

2.3 **Quality Traveler & Inspection Sign-Off:** Subcontractor shall complete and sign a formal Quality Traveler sheet for each panel. A copy of the completed traveler and FAT sign-off report must accompany the shipping documentation and be transmitted digitally to Contractor prior to invoicing.

2.4 **Inspection Access:** Contractor and its designated client representatives reserve the right to inspect panel assembly and witness FAT testing at Subcontractor's facility during normal business hours with 24 hours advance notice.

---

### Section 3: Compensation and Milestone Payment Terms

3.1 **Labor Rates and Material Pass-Through:** Unless otherwise specified in an SOW:
(a) Panel wiring and mechanical assembly labor shall be billed at the agreed contract shop rate of $[Rate]/hour.  
(b) Serialized UL 508A listing labels shall be billed at a flat fee of $[Label Fee] per serialized label.  
(c) Sourced materials provided by Subcontractor shall be billed at actual landed cost plus [Agreed]% markup. Sourced materials procured directly by Contractor shall bear no markup.  

3.2 **Payment Terms:** Payment terms are Net 30 days following receipt of an undisputed invoice, complete FAT traveler documentation, and confirmed physical receipt of panels in acceptable condition.

3.3 **Withholding for Defective Work:** Contractor reserves the right to withhold payment on any panel failing FAT or delivered with unapproved deviations until such deficiencies are cured by Subcontractor at Subcontractor's sole expense.

---

### Section 4: Indemnification and Idaho Statutory Compliance

4.1 **Idaho Code § 29-114 Compliance:** The Parties explicitly acknowledge and agree that this Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Idaho. Pursuant to Idaho Code § 29-114, any provision purporting to indemnify the promisee against liability for damages arising out of bodily injury or damage to property resulting from the sole negligence or willful misconduct of the promisee or its agents is void.

4.2 **Subcontractor Indemnity:** Subject to Section 4.1, Subcontractor agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Contractor, its officers, members, and clients from and against any and all claims, damages, liabilities, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney fees) to the extent caused by the negligent acts, errors, omissions, breach of contract, or intentional misconduct of Subcontractor, its employees, or agents in the performance of work under this Agreement.

4.3 **Contractor Indemnity:** Contractor agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Subcontractor from and against claims and damages to the extent caused by the negligent acts or intentional misconduct of Contractor or Contractor's defective engineering designs where Subcontractor strictly followed approved prints without deviation.

---

### Section 5: Back-to-Back Workmanship Warranty

5.1 **Workmanship Warranty Period:** Subcontractor warrants that all panels, wiring, mechanical assemblies, and fabrication work furnished under this Agreement will be free from defects in workmanship, assembly, and fabrication for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of final commissioning or eighteen (18) months from shipment, whichever occurs first ("Warranty Period").

5.2 **Defect Remediation:** In the event of any defect in workmanship (including loose terminal terminations, improper wire crimps, component miswiring, or non-compliance with UL 508A rules) discovered during the Warranty Period:
(a) Subcontractor shall, within forty-eight (48) hours of notice, dispatch qualified personnel to remediate the defect on-site, or  
(b) Subcontractor shall authorize Contractor or a third party to execute emergency repairs, and Subcontractor shall reimburse Contractor for all reasonable costs incurred.  

5.3 **Manufacturer Component Warranties:** Subcontractor shall pass through to Contractor and end-clients all standard original equipment manufacturer (OEM) warranties for components integrated into panels (e.g., Rockwell Automation, Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric, Saginaw, Hoffman).

---

### Section 6: Delivery, Risk of Loss, and Liquidated Schedule Flow-Down

6.1 **Delivery Terms:** All deliveries shall be FOB Subcontractor Facility unless otherwise agreed in writing. Risk of loss passes to Contractor upon tender of panels to the designated freight carrier, provided packaging meets NEMA transport standards.

6.2 **Project Schedule Criticality:** Subcontractor acknowledges that industrial automation projects and data center deployments involve strict construction milestone schedules. Subcontractor shall maintain adequate wiring technician staffing to meet SOW delivery commitments.

6.3 **Notice of Delay:** If Subcontractor anticipates any delay due to component shortages or labor constraints, Subcontractor must notify Contractor in writing within twenty-four (24) hours, stating the cause and a concrete mitigation schedule.

---

### Section 7: Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation

7.1 **Proprietary Information:** All schematics, drawings, BOMs, client identities, pricing terms, and technical specifications provided by Contractor remain the exclusive intellectual property of Contractor. Subcontractor shall not disclose or use such information except as strictly necessary to fulfill this Agreement.

7.2 **Non-Solicitation of End-Clients:** Subcontractor agrees that during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following its termination, Subcontractor will not directly or indirectly solicit, quote, or perform industrial control panel fabrication or automation services for any end-client, general contractor, or engineering firm introduced to Subcontractor by Contractor, without prior written authorization from Contractor.

---

### Section 8: Commercial Insurance Requirements

8.1 **Minimum Insurance Coverage:** Prior to commencing any work, Subcontractor shall furnish to Contractor Certificates of Insurance (COI) demonstrating the following active coverages with an A.M. Best rating of A- VII or better:

(a) **Commercial General Liability (CGL):**  
- $1,000,000 each occurrence  
- $2,000,000 general aggregate  
- $2,000,000 products and completed operations aggregate  
- ISO Form CG 20 10 04 13 (ongoing) and CG 20 37 04 13 (completed operations) endorsements.  

(b) **Worker's Compensation & Employer's Liability:**  
- Statutory limits under Idaho law  
- $1,000,000 each accident / disease / policy limit.  

(c) **Errors and Omissions / Professional Liability (if providing drafting/engineering):**  
- $1,000,000 each claim and aggregate.  

(d) **Commercial Auto Liability:**  
- $1,000,000 combined single limit.  

8.2 **Additional Insured Endorsement:** Sawtooth Industrial Systems LLC shall be named as an **Additional Insured** on a primary and non-contributory basis on Subcontractor's CGL and Auto policies. A Waiver of Subrogation in favor of Contractor shall apply to all policies.

8.3 **Notice of Cancellation:** Policies shall provide for thirty (30) days prior written notice to Contractor before cancellation or non-renewal.

---

### Section 9: Term and Termination

9.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall take effect on the Effective Date and remain in effect for a period of three (3) years, renewing automatically for one-year increments unless terminated in writing.

9.2 **Termination for Convenience:** Contractor may terminate this Agreement or any open SOW for convenience upon fourteen (14) days written notice. In such event, Contractor shall compensate Subcontractor for completed work and non-cancelable material commitments incurred up to the date of notice.

9.3 **Termination for Cause:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party:  
(a) Materially breaches any provision and fails to cure such breach within ten (10) business days;  
(b) Loses its UL 508A panel shop certification or necessary operational licenses;  
(c) Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy protection.  

---

### Section 10: General Provisions

10.1 **Independent Contractor:** The relationship of Subcontractor to Contractor is strictly that of an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship. Subcontractor exercises complete discretion over its employees, work methods, and facility operations.

10.2 **Governing Law and Dispute Resolution:** This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Idaho. Any legal action or proceeding arising out of this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Ada County, Idaho.

10.3 **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, and all remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10.4 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with all executed SOWs, represents the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
