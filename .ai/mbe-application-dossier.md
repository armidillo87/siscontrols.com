## Section D: Operating Model, Facilities, and Technical Sourcing Architecture

### Question D.1: Explain your operational facility setup, physical operations base, and execution model.
**Applicant Response:**  
Sawtooth Industrial Systems LLC operates from its commercial business office in Boise, Ada County, Idaho (Zip: 83713). The Boise headquarters houses our engineering workstations, electrical CAD drafting tools, component catalog library, estimating engines, and administrative management.

To provide maximum agility without the capital drag of an idle single-line factory, Sawtooth utilizes a hybrid integration model:
- **In-House Core Functions:** Engineering specification, component cross-referencing, BOM optimization, wholesale trade procurement, quality control auditing, and customer relationship management are executed directly by Sawtooth in Boise.
- **Contract Assembly & UL Certification:** Physical panel wiring, point-to-point harness assembly, and serialized UL 508A label application are executed through vetted, certified regional UL 508A partner panel shops pursuant to strict Subcontractor Master Services Agreements. Sawtooth supplies the engineering prints, procures or specifies the bill of materials, and maintains technical authority over the completed product.

This structure complies fully with the Non-Manufacturer Rule (13 CFR § 121.406) and qualifies under prime contractor Tier-2 small business subcontracting plans (FAR 19.7) as a specialized industrial sourcing and systems supplier.
