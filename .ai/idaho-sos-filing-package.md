## Part 1: Step-by-Step Idaho SOSBiz Online Filing Walkthrough

Follow these exact steps to complete the legal name amendment online:

1. **Access SOSBiz:**  
   Navigate to [https://sosbiz.idaho.gov](https://sosbiz.idaho.gov) and log in with your existing account credentials.
2. **Locate Existing Entity:**  
   In the dashboard, select **"My Records"** or search business entities for **"Sawtooth Distribution LLC"**.

   Click the entity record and select **"File an Amendment"** -> **"Certificate of Amendment of Limited Liability Company"**.

   - Proposed New Entity Name: `Sawtooth Industrial Systems LLC`  
   *(The system will perform an instantaneous real-time name availability check. The name is confirmed available and compliant with Idaho Code § 30-25-112 requiring the designation "Limited Liability Company" or "LLC".)*
5. **Complete Section 2 (Management Structure Confirmation):**  
   - Select: **"Manager-Managed Limited Liability Company"**  
   - Designate Sole Manager:  
     - Full Legal Name: Brandon  
     - Title: Managing Member & General Manager  
     - Business Address: Boise, Idaho 83713  
6. **Confirm Registered Agent:**  
   Confirm your existing Commercial Registered Agent or individual Registered Agent address in Idaho.
7. **Electronic Execution:**  
   - Signer Name: Brandon  
   - Title: Managing Member  
   - Attestation: Check the box certifying that the amendment was duly authorized by 100% of the membership interests.
8. **Payment & Receipt:**

## Part 3: State Sales Tax Exemption Filing Instructions

To purchase electrical components, enclosures, breakers, and wire wholesale without paying 6.0% Idaho state sales tax or 4.85% Utah sales tax, provide the following executed certificates to Platt, Crescent, CED, and Graybar:

### Idaho Exemption: Form ST-101 (Sales Tax Resale or Exemption Certificate)
1. **Download:** Obtain Form ST-101 from the Idaho State Tax Commission ([tax.idaho.gov](https://tax.idaho.gov)).
2. **Buyer Section:**  
   - Buyer's Name: `Sawtooth Industrial Systems LLC`  
   - Address: Boise, ID 83713  
   - Idaho Seller's Permit or IBR Number: [Enter your 9-digit Idaho Business Registration Number]
3. **Exemption Reason:**  
   - Check Box: **"Resale"** (Idaho Code § 63-3622D)  
   - Nature of Business: *Industrial automation equipment and control panel integration.*  
   - Description of Goods Purchased: *Electrical enclosures, circuit breakers, contactors, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), variable frequency drives (VFDs), power distribution components, and industrial wiring.*
4. **Sign & Deliver:** Sign and email the PDF to your wholesale distributor credit representative with each credit application.

### Utah Exemption: Form TC-721 (Exemption Certificate)
*(Required when sourcing components out of Salt Lake City distribution hubs like Codale or Platt SLC Central)*
1. **Download:** Obtain Form TC-721 from the Utah State Tax Commission ([tax.utah.gov](https://tax.utah.gov)).
2. **Exemption Box:** Select **Resale or Component Part of Manufactured Goods**.
3. **Sign & Deliver:** File with Utah branch distributors to ensure zero tax withholding on shipped freight.
